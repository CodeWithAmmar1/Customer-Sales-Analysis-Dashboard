# Customer Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Data Analytics dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze customer shopping behavior, revenue trends, payment preferences, demographic distribution, and mall performance.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Category Performance:** **Clothing** dominates total sales revenue, followed by **Shoes** and **Technology**. **Cosmetics** and **Toys** represent smaller revenue shares.
* **Customer Demographics:** **Female shoppers** drive the majority of sales (**60%**), compared to **Male shoppers** (**40%**).
* **Payment Preferences:** **Cash** is the most preferred payment method (~112M), followed by **Credit Cards** (~88M) and **Debit Cards** (~50M).
* **Top Performing Malls:** **Mall of Istanbul** (50.9M) and **Kanyon** (50.6M) lead customer sales among all locations.
* **Seasonality & Trends:** Sales revenue and order volume peak highest at the beginning of the year (**January & February**), remaining steady across the subsequent months.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

1. **Top 5 Sales by Category:** Column chart showing overall revenue generated per product category.
2. **Sales by Payment Method:** Bar/Trend visual displaying revenue split across Cash, Credit, and Debit cards.
3. **Top 5 Malls by Customer Sales:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting the top revenue-generating retail locations.
4. **Orders vs. Sales:** Dual-axis chart comparing monthly order counts against total sales revenue.
5. **Demographic Split (Men vs. Women):** Pie chart illustrating gender contribution.
6. **Yearly Comparison:** Multi-year clustered bar chart evaluating month-over-month performance across 2021, 2022, and 2023.
7. **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic Year and Month filtering for real-time analysis.
